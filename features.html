THE CLUB

Built around participation.

Stellar Science Club combines a science-focused Discord community with YouTube study content, events and student-led projects.

🔬
SCIENCE

Subject channels

Physics, chemistry, biology, astronomy, engineering and computer science all have dedicated places to talk.

📚
STUDY

Study together

Ask for help, share resources, join focused study rooms and use the club's YouTube sessions when you need them.

🎯
CHALLENGES

Make learning active

Weekly challenges, quizzes, project showcases and discussions give members something to contribute.

🎓
JMSS

JMSS preparation

Dedicated channels for John Monash Science School applicants and students, including entry-focused discussion.

📺
YOUTUBE

Content outside Discord

Study sessions and science videos give the community a public-facing place to learn and discover the club.

🤝
COMMUNITY

Student-led

The culture is built around students helping students, sharing ideas and making the server better together.
